# Documentation

Start with the [project README](../README.md). These files show how the site is made and checked.

## The evidence

- [claims.md](claims.md): every factual statement on the site, its kind, its sources, and the pages that use it. Generated.
- [sources.md](sources.md): every source and link, the date on it, when it was checked, and whether it was read. Generated.
- [corrections.md](corrections.md): where official sources disagree, and what the site does about it.
- [research-log.md](research-log.md): what was read, what could not be checked, and why.

## The rules

- [methodology.md](methodology.md): where facts may come from and how disagreements are settled.
- [style-guide.md](style-guide.md): the Smart Brevity page pattern and word rules.
- [checker-logic.md](checker-logic.md): every input and result of the 1-minute check.
- [boundaries.md](boundaries.md): what the project will not do.

## Access and privacy

- [accessibility.md](accessibility.md): standards, cognitive accessibility patterns, known gaps.
- [design.md](design.md): ODHS palette roles, themes, type, and components.
- [privacy.md](privacy.md): data flows and how the Content Security Policy enforces them.

## Proof it works

- [testing.md](testing.md): what was tested and what was not.
- [testing-browser.md](testing-browser.md), [contrast.md](contrast.md), [reading-level.md](reading-level.md): generated reports.

## Keeping it current

- [maintenance.md](maintenance.md): what runs on its own, the Cloudflare Pages settings, and what a person must do.
- [../data/review.json](../data/review.json): dated checkpoints.
- [roadmap.md](roadmap.md): what comes next.
- [decisions/](decisions/): why it was built this way. [0013](decisions/0013-utah-section.md) explains the Utah guide, and [0014](decisions/0014-guide-list.md) explains how the build tells guides apart.
- [adding-a-guide.md](adding-a-guide.md): the steps for adding another state.
