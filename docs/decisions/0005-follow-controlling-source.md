# 0005. When sources disagree, follow the controlling one and say so

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-02

## Context

On Nov. 2, 2026, several OHA documents predated the June 3, 2026 federal rule or the Oct. 29, 2026 court ruling, and a few disagreed with each other (see [../corrections.md](../corrections.md)).

## Decision

Follow the source order in [../methodology.md](../methodology.md): federal statute, federal rule, court orders, Oregon rules, then agency guidance, newest first. Disclose every disagreement in the corrections log, with what the site chose.

## Consequences

- In a few places the site's wording differs from an OHA fact sheet, for example "$580 or more" and the school definition.
- Applicants might see different wording on a state document. The corrections log explains why, and the site points to OHA for the final word on any individual case.
