# 0007. No machine translation; link to the state's languages

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-02

## Context

A wrong translation of an eligibility rule can cost someone coverage. The state already offers the paper application in 17 languages, phone lines in many languages, and free interpreters.

## Decision

- The site is in English for 0.1.0.
- The help page links to every language version of the paper application, with native-language names marked up correctly, and to the state's language lines.
- Future translations will be done or reviewed by people from each language community.

## Consequences

- Non-English readers depend on the state's materials and phone lines, or on their browser's translation, until reviewed translations exist.
