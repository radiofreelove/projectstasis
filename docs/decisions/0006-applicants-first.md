# 0006. Applicants first

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-02

## Context

The rules affect both new applicants (starting Jan. 1, 2027) and current members (at renewals, mostly from 2027 into 2028). Trying to serve both at once would double the content and the chance of mistakes.

## Decision

Release 0.1.0 is written for people applying, including people applying again after being turned down. Renewal guidance is on the roadmap with a checkpoint on 2027-08-01.

## Consequences

- Current members may find some pages useful (letters, reasons, help) but will not find renewal timelines yet.
