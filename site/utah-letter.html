Utah sent me a letter. Now what?

This page explains the letters and how to answer them on time.

The short version

  • First: Find the due date on the letter.
  • Who writes: When you need to act, the Department of Workforce Services (DWS) sends a letter made for you.
  • Proof needed? You may need to send a form within 30 days, or you could lose your coverage.
  • Do not skip it: You can lose Medicaid if you do not return forms or you miss a due date.
  • How to answer: Online, by mail, by fax, or in person.

Utah printed each letter on paper of a different color.

Green letter

Ways to send papers

  • Online: upload them in myCase at jobs.utah.gov/mycase
  • Mail: Department of Workforce Services, Imaging Operations, PO Box 143245, Salt Lake City, UT 84114-3245
  • Fax: 1-877-313-4717 or 801-526-9500
  • In person: any DWS employment center, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Check your own letter first. If it lists a different address or fax number, use that one.

Tips

  • Name and case number: Write your name and case number on every page you send.
  • Keep copies: Keep a copy of everything you send, and write down the date you sent it.
  • Do not wait: Answer as soon as you can. Mail takes time.
  • Stay reachable: Keep your address, phone number, and email current in myCase.

Missed the due date?

If you miss a due date, you can lose Medicaid. You can apply again. See your options
