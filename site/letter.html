OHP sent me a letter. Now what?

This page explains the letters and how to answer them on time.

The short version

  • First: Find the due date on the letter.
  • Proof needed? A Request for Information means OHP needs more from you. You have 35 days to reply.
  • Reminder: If you signed up for email, you get a reminder 5 days before it is due.
  • How to answer: Online, by phone, in person, by fax, or by mail.

Letters you may get

Notice of Eligibility
OHP's decision about your coverage.
Request for Information
OHP needs proof or more facts. The due date is on the letter. You have 35 days.
Notice of Status
Your status for the new rules changed. Your benefits did not change. It helps you get ready for your next renewal.

Ways to answer

  • Online: your ONE account at benefits.oregon.gov
  • Phone: 800-699-9075 (TTY 711)
  • In person: any local ODHS office
  • Fax: 503-378-5628
  • Mail: PO Box 14015, Salem, OR 97309. Check your letter for the exact address.
  • With help: a free community partner

Tips

  • Keep copies: Keep a copy of everything you send, and write down the date you sent it.
  • Case number: Write the case number from your letter on every page you send.
  • Do not wait: Answer as soon as you can. Mail takes time.

Missed the due date?

If you do not send proof by the due date, OHP can turn you down. You can apply again any time. See your options
