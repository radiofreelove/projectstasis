Why this site exists

Oregon, Washington, and Utah each explain their new Medicaid rules across many pages and papers. This site puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words, with one guide for each state.

Independent. Not run by Oregon, Washington, or Utah, or by any of their agencies. Only the state decides who qualifies.
