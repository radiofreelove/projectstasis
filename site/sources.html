  54. SMD# 26-006, Optional Hold Harmless Period for Community Engagement Implementation (State Medicaid Director letter) PDF
    Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. Date on it: Nov. 13, 2026. Checked: 2026-11-14. Read by us: Yes.
    Why we list it: Read in full on 2026-11-14: 4 pages, 3 footnotes, signed by Daniel Brillman, Deputy Administrator and Director. Offers every state an optional hold harmless period through June 30, 2027. A state that elects it may suspend denials of eligibility and disenrollments when an applicable individual does not demonstrate compliance with community engagement. This covers applications received and renewals initiated through and including June 30, 2027. A state may implement a shorter period. A state may use the period only if it assesses whether each individual meets the requirement (including deemed compliance) or is excluded, for all relevant applications and renewals in the period. The period covers only conditioning eligibility on community engagement (section 1902(xx)(1) of the Act, 42 C.F.R. 435.559); it does not apply to any other part of implementing community engagement by Jan. 1, 2027, or to denials and disenrollments on other eligibility criteria, such as age or household income. A state requests the period in its mitigation plan and must have CMS approval before using it; a state that intends to use it from Jan. 1, 2027 is asked to notify CMS by Dec. 11, 2026. For applications submitted and renewals initiated after the period ends in a state, the state must deny or terminate eligibility for not meeting the requirement, after the noncompliance procedures at 42 C.F.R. 435.558.
