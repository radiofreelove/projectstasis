Why this site exists

The state explains the new OHP rules across more than a dozen documents. This site puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists the official sources behind it.

Independent. Not run by the State of Oregon, the Oregon Health Authority (OHA), or the Oregon Department of Human Services (ODHS). Only OHP decides who qualifies.

Private. No accounts, ads, cookies, or tracking. How your privacy is protected.
