Every source we use

This is the full list of sources and links behind the Oregon Health Plan guide. Each one shows the date on it and the date we checked it.

  • Read by us: "Yes" means we read the document itself. "No" means we got the address from another official source and link to it.
  • Fact log: To see which fact comes from which source, read the public fact log.
  • Disagreements: Where official papers say different things, see the corrections log.

Why this site exists

The state explains the new OHP rules across more than a dozen documents. This site puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists the official sources behind it.

Independent. Not run by the State of Oregon, the Oregon Health Authority (OHA), or the Oregon Department of Human Services (ODHS). Only OHP decides who qualifies.

Private. No accounts, ads, cookies, or tracking. How your privacy is protected.
