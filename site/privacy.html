Your privacy

This site is built so your answers stay with you.

The short version

  • Nothing to sign up for: No accounts, ads, cookies, or tracking.
  • Your answers stay here: The 1-minute check runs on your device. Your answers are never saved or sent.
  • Display settings: Text size and colors are saved on this device only. Use "Reset to standard" to clear them.
  • The host: Cloudflare hosts this site. To run it, Cloudflare handles visitors' IP addresses.

How we keep your answers private

  • Our code never sends your answers anywhere. As a second lock, each page tells your browser to block the page from sending any data.
  • When you click a link to a state website, your browser does not tell that site which of our pages you came from.
  • The check never asks about immigration status.
  • We do not load fonts, images, or code from other companies.

For the technical reader

Every page comes with a Content Security Policy that has connect-src 'none' and form-action 'none'. The host sends it, and the page repeats it. Scripts and styles load only from this site. frame-ancestors 'none' stops other sites from showing these pages inside their own. You can check all of this in your browser's developer tools.

Display settings use one localStorage key, stasis-display-v1. Nothing else is stored.

Full details are in our public privacy notes.

Telling us about a problem

Problem reports go to GitHub and are public. Do not share your name, case number, health, immigration status, or other personal details.

Why this site exists

The state explains the new OHP rules across more than a dozen documents. This site puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists the official sources behind it.
