Free help with Utah Medicaid, and other languages

People who can help you apply, for free, and in your language.

The short version

  • Call the state: The Department of Workforce Services (DWS) answers questions about who qualifies at 1-866-435-7414.
  • Free helpers: Take Care Utah helps people apply, renew, and answer notices. Its help is free.
  • Your language: Language help is free.
  • Paper application: It comes in English and Spanish.

Help in person

  • Find a DWS office near you
  • Make an appointment with Take Care Utah. It says its help is offered across the state.

Other formats

If you have a disability, you can ask for aids and services that help you use DWS. They are free. Call 801-526-9240.

Help in your language

  • Utah Medicaid says free language help is available to you. The application says translation help is available while you apply.
  • For a hearing, a free interpreter can be arranged.
  • Utah mailed its 2026 letters about the work rules in English and Spanish.

Forms in Spanish

  • Medical application, form 61MED, in Spanish PDF
  • Solicitud de audiencia imparcial (hearing request, form 490, in Spanish) PDF

Why this guide exists

Utah explains the new Medicaid rules across more than a dozen pages and papers. This guide puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists the official sources behind it.
