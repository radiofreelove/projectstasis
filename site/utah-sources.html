Every source we use

This is the full list of sources and links behind the Utah Medicaid guide. Each one shows the date on it and the date we checked it.

  • Read by us: "Yes" means we read the document itself. "No" means we got the address from another official source and link to it.
  • How we read Utah's: On Nov. 10, 2026, with a research tool. A person has not yet read each one from start to finish.
  • Fact log: To see which fact comes from which source, read the public fact log.
  • Disagreements: Where official papers say different things, see the corrections log.

Why this guide exists

Utah explains the new Medicaid rules across more than a dozen pages and papers. This guide puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists the official sources behind it.

Independent. Not run by the State of Utah, the Utah Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), or the Utah Department of Workforce Services (DWS). Only the state decides who qualifies.

Private. No accounts, ads, cookies, or tracking. How your privacy is protected.
