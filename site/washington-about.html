Why this guide exists

Big changes to Apple Health start Jan. 1, 2027. The official facts are real, but they are spread out. This guide brings them together for people who are applying.

The short version

  • The problem: The facts are spread across many official pages and papers. Our list has 9 from the state that explain the changes.
  • Why it matters: New federal work rules for Apple Health for Adults start Jan. 1, 2027.
  • Good to know: Washington checks its own data first. The Health Care Authority (HCA) estimated that it could check about 64 percent of people in Apple Health for Adults this way, or find they are exempt.
  • What we do: Put what applicants need in one place, in plain words, with the official source for every page.

Three guides

  • Washington: You are in the Apple Health guide. It was added on Nov. 12, 2026.
  • Oregon: The Oregon Health Plan (OHP) guide came first. It has its own pages, its own check, and its own sources.
  • Utah: The Utah Medicaid guide was added on Nov. 10, 2026. It has its own pages, its own check, and its own sources.
  • Same method: All 3 guides use the same rules for facts, sources, plain words, and privacy.

What this guide is

  • A free guide for people applying for Apple Health.
  • Short pages, plain words, and big buttons.
  • Links to the state's own pages, so you always use the real thing.

What this guide is not

  • Not the state. Not run by Washington's government, the Health Care Authority (HCA), or DSHS.
  • Not an application. You apply with the state.
  • Not a decision. This guide says "you may." Only Washington decides who meets the rules or is exempt.
  • Not legal advice.

This guide is new

We built this guide on Nov. 12, 2026, from research notes. A person has not yet read each source from start to finish. Some sources were read only in part. If this guide and the state say different things, follow the state, and tell us.

How we keep it right

  1. We link to official forms and pages instead of copying them.
  2. A robot checks every official link each week and flags broken ones.
  3. Every fact on this site is listed in a public fact log with its source.
  4. When official pages disagree, we say so in public.
