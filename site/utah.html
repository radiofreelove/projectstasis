Applying for Utah Medicaid? New rules start Jan. 1, 2027.

Why this guide exists: Utah explains the new Medicaid rules across more than a dozen pages and papers. We have found 18 so far. This guide puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists its official sources.

Have medical bills? Read this before Jan. 1, 2027.

Through Dec. 31, 2026, most Utah Medicaid programs can cover care from up to 3 months before the month you apply.

Starting Jan. 1, 2027, Adult Expansion Medicaid can cover care from 1 month before the month you apply.

More about past medical bills

The short version

  • What's new: Starting Jan. 1, 2027, some adults ages 19 to 64 must meet new work or activity rules to get Utah Medicaid.
  • Which programs: Adult Expansion Medicaid and Targeted Adult Medicaid (TAM).
  • Utah's name for the rules: the community engagement requirement.
  • When the state looks: The month before you apply.
  • Records first: The state checks records it already has before it asks you for anything.

Meet the rules any one way

  • Income: You earn at least $580 a month.
  • School: You go to school at least half-time.
  • 80 hours: You spend 80 hours a month on work, job training, volunteering, or school. Add them up.

The rules, in plain words

You may not have to if you

  • Are pregnant, or get Medicaid for the months after a birth
  • Care for a child 13 or younger, or for a person with a disability
  • Have a disability, or a health condition that makes daily tasks hard
  • Are American Indian or Alaska Native
  • Are in foster care, or were

There are more reasons. Utah calls them exclusions and exceptions. See every reason

What to do

  1. Take the 1-minute check. Your answers stay on your device.
  2. Apply online, on paper, or in person.
  3. If the state cannot confirm your activity or your reason from its records, you can report it yourself.

Bottom line

If you think you qualify, apply. If the state says no, you can apply again.
