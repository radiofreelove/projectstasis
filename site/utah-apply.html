How to apply for Utah Medicaid

There are 3 ways to apply. Pick the one that is easiest for you.

The short version

  • Online: in myCase, at jobs.utah.gov/mycase
  • On paper: the medical application, form 61MED, in English or Spanish. Mail it or fax it.
  • In person: at any Department of Workforce Services (DWS) office.
  • Questions: Call DWS at 1-866-435-7414.
  • Free help: Take Care Utah helps people apply. Its help is free.

Who decides

DWS decides who qualifies for Utah Medicaid.

Apply online

Go to jobs.utah.gov/mycase. If you apply online, you do not need a paper application.

Apply on paper

Download the medical application (form 61MED) PDF. It also comes in Spanish PDF.

  • Mail it to: Department of Workforce Services, PO Box 143245, Salt Lake City, UT 84114-3245.
  • Or fax it: Utah's pages list different fax numbers. Use the number printed on your form, or call DWS to check.
  • No printer? Call 1-866-435-7414 and ask DWS to mail you an application. This can take longer.

Heads up about the paper form

The paper form we read is dated April 1, 2026. It does not ask about the new rules. The state may ask you about them after you apply.

Apply in person

Find a DWS office near you. DWS calls its offices employment centers.

Utah's letters say you can drop off papers Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

What to have ready

  • DWS tells you what proof it needs, like a birth certificate.
  • DWS sends a form that lists what it needs. Return those items within 10 days.
  • For the new rules: your work, school, or volunteer hours for the month before you apply, or the reason you don't have to meet the rules.

What happens next

  1. The state checks records it already has first.
  2. If the state needs more for the new rules, you may need to send a form within 30 days, or you could lose your coverage. How to answer a letter
  3. DWS has 30 days to decide. If you say you have a disability, it has 90 days. You get the decision in a letter.

Help in your language

Language help is free. Ask DWS when you call or visit. More free help and phone numbers

Good to know

  • Income limits change. Check the income table on Utah's Medicaid expansion page.
  • Does your job offer health insurance? Utah Medicaid may require you to sign up for it. Medicaid then pays back your share of the monthly cost.
  • Keep your address, phone number, and email current in myCase, so you do not miss a letter.

Why this site exists

Utah explains the new Medicaid rules across more than a dozen pages and papers. This guide puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists the official sources behind it.
