Free help and other languages

People who can help you apply, for free, and in your language.

The short version

  • Call OHP: 800-699-9075 (TTY 711), Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • Free helpers: Community partners can help you apply. Their help is free.
  • Your language: There are phone lines for many languages.
  • Paper application: It comes in 17 languages.

Other formats

You can ask OHP for letters and papers in other languages, large print, braille, or another format. It is free. Call 800-699-9075.

Why this site exists

The state explains the new OHP rules across more than a dozen documents. This site puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists the official sources behind it.

Independent. Not run by the State of Oregon, the Oregon Health Authority (OHA), or the Oregon Department of Human Services (ODHS). Only OHP decides who qualifies.

Private. No accounts, ads, cookies, or tracking. How your privacy is protected.
