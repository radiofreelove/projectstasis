Hours log you can print

Write down your hours. Keep the page in case the state asks.

Please read

  • This is not a Utah Medicaid form. It is for your own records.
  • The state decides what proof it accepts.
  • Utah has its own form for reporting, form 742. Ask the state for it.
  • When you apply, the state looks at the month before you apply.
  • The goal is 80 hours in a month. Work, job training, volunteering, and school all add up.

Why this guide exists

Utah explains the new Medicaid rules across more than a dozen pages and papers. This guide puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists the official sources behind it.

Independent. Not run by the State of Utah, the Utah Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), or the Utah Department of Workforce Services (DWS). Only the state decides who qualifies.
