Why this guide exists

Big changes to Utah Medicaid start Jan. 1, 2027. The official facts are real, but they are spread out. This guide brings them together for people who are applying.

The short version

  • The problem: Utah explains the new rules across more than a dozen pages and papers. We have found 18 so far.
  • Why it matters: In January 2026, Utah said about 83,000 people were in the 2 programs the new rules cover.
  • Yes, but: Many Utah pages show no date. A few say different things. We tell you where, and we tell you what to ask the state.
  • What we do: Put what applicants need in one place, in plain words, with the official source for every page.

Three guides

  • Utah: You are in the Utah Medicaid guide. It was added on Nov. 10, 2026.
  • Oregon: The Oregon Health Plan (OHP) guide came first. It has its own pages, its own check, and its own sources.
  • Washington: The Apple Health guide was added on Nov. 12, 2026. It has its own pages, its own check, and its own sources.
  • Same method: All 3 guides use the same rules for facts, sources, plain words, and privacy.

What this guide is

  • A free guide for people applying for Utah Medicaid.
  • Short pages, plain words, and big buttons.
  • Links to the state's own forms and pages, so you always use the real thing.

What this guide is not

  • Not the state. Not run by the State of Utah, the Utah Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), or the Utah Department of Workforce Services (DWS).
  • Not an application. You apply with the state.
  • Not a decision. Only the state decides who qualifies.
  • Not legal advice. For that, see free legal help.

This guide is new

We read Utah's pages and papers on Nov. 10, 2026, with a research tool. A person has not yet read each one from start to finish. If this guide and the state say different things, follow the state, and tell us.

How we keep it right

  1. We link to official forms and pages instead of copying them.
  2. A robot checks every official link each week and flags broken ones.
  3. Every fact on this site is listed in a public fact log with its source.
  4. When official pages disagree, we say so in public.

Curious how it is made? Everything is public: the code, the fact log, and every decision.
