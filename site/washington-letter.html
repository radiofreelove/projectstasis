Washington sent me a letter. Now what?

This page explains Apple Health letters and how to answer them on time.

The short version

  • First: Find the due date on the letter.
  • How notices come: By mail or email. The Health Care Authority (HCA) uses the choice in your file.
  • Answer it: If a letter asks for proof, you must answer. That is true even if you just updated your information.
  • How long: The letter gives you 15 days. Coverage may close if the proof does not arrive.
  • About the work rules: HCA says there is nothing you need to do right now.

Letters that ask for proof

After you apply or renew, a letter may ask for proof.

Your letter says what to send and how to send it. Follow your letter. HCA's page has more: Submit my verification.

How HCA planned to reach people

HCA planned to reach people through letters, a web page for clients, text messages, videos, and paid ads. Part of that plan depended on extra money in the state budget.

Tips

  • Keep copies: Keep a copy of everything you send, and write down the date you sent it.
  • Do not wait: Answer as soon as you can. Mail takes time.
  • Stay reachable: Keep your contact information up to date, so notices reach you.

Questions?

Call HCA's Medical Eligibility Determination Services (MEDS) at 1-800-562-3022.

Missed the due date?

If proof does not arrive, coverage may close. See your options
