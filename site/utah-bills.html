Medical bills from before you apply for Utah Medicaid

Utah Medicaid can cover some care from before you apply. Starting Jan. 1, 2027, that window is shorter.

The short version

  • Through Dec. 31, 2026: Most programs can cover care from up to 3 months before the month you apply.
  • Starting Jan. 1, 2027: 1 month before the month you apply for Adult Expansion Medicaid. 2 months for most other Medicaid programs.
  • Utah's word for it: retroactive coverage.
  • You ask for it: Utah describes this as coverage you can request for care you got before you applied.

What this means for you

Have unpaid medical bills from the last few months? Applying sooner may help. How to apply

Questions about a bill? Call the Department of Workforce Services at 1-866-435-7414.
