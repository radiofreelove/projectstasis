How to apply for Apple Health

There are 4 ways to apply. Pick the one that is easiest for you.

The short version

  • Online: at wahealthplanfinder.org. The site is called Washington Healthplanfinder.
  • By phone: Call the Healthplanfinder Customer Support Center at 1-855-923-4633.
  • On paper: the Application for health care coverage, form 18-001P.
  • In person: A navigator can help you. The help is free.
  • Any time: Apple Health enrollment is open all year.

First, pick the right door

  • Adults under 65 without Medicare: Apply through Washington Healthplanfinder. The ways are on this page.
  • Age 65 or older, blind, have a disability, or have Medicare: That program is called Apple Health Classic. You apply through DSHS. Use Washington Connection online, or call 1-877-501-2233.

Apply online

Go to wahealthplanfinder.org. You can apply or renew there.

The Health Care Authority (HCA) explains each way on its page: Apply for or renew coverage.

Apply by phone

Call the Healthplanfinder Customer Support Center at 1-855-923-4633. The TTY number is 1-855-627-9604.

Apply on paper

The paper form is the Application for health care coverage, form 18-001P. You can fax it to 1-855-867-4467.

HCA's page has more about the form and where to send it: Filing an application.

Apply in person

A navigator can help you apply in person. The help is free. More free help

What happens next

  1. Washington checks its own data first.
  2. After you apply, a letter may ask for proof. It gives you 15 days. Coverage may close if the proof does not arrive. How to answer a letter
  3. Have a question about your case? Call HCA's Medical Eligibility Determination Services (MEDS) at 1-800-562-3022.

Help in your language

Language help is free. Help in your language

If something changes

You can report a change in Washington Healthplanfinder. Look for "Report a Change in Income or Household." You can also report a change by phone, mail, or fax.
