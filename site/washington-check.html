How this check works

The check runs on this page only. It uses the same lists as our pages on the Apple Health rules and the reasons you may not have to meet them. It does not ask about your county. You can read every rule it uses in our public checker notes.

The 5 ways in step 3 are the 5 on the fact sheet from the Health Care Authority (HCA). That list does not have hours at a paid job as a way of its own. It names work hours only as part of a mix.

Why this guide exists

The facts about the new Apple Health rules are spread across many official pages and papers. This guide puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists the official sources behind it.
