Medical bills from before you apply

OHP can help with some bills from before you apply. Starting Jan. 1, 2027, that window is shorter for most adults.

The short version

  • Through Dec. 31, 2026: OHP can help with bills from up to 3 months before you apply.
  • Starting Jan. 1, 2027: 1 month for most adults ages 19 to 64. Up to 2 months for everyone else.
  • OHP's word for it: retroactive coverage.

Who gets up to 2 months starting Jan. 1, 2027

  • Children
  • People over 65
  • People who are pregnant
  • People who get OHP because of a disability

What this means for you

Have unpaid medical bills from the last few months? Applying sooner may help. How to apply
