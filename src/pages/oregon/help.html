Free help and other languages

People who can help you apply, for free, and in your language.

The short version

  • Call OHP: 800-699-9075 (TTY 711), Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • Free helpers: Community partners can help you apply. Their help is free.
  • Your language: There are phone lines for many languages.
  • Paper application: It comes in 17 languages.
