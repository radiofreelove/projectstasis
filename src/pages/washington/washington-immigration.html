Immigration status and Apple Health

A federal law changed who can get federally funded Medicaid on Oct. 1, 2026. This page explains what Washington has said.

The short version

  • What changed: From Oct. 1, 2026, fewer noncitizen adults can get federally funded Medicaid.
  • Other programs: The Health Care Authority (HCA) names 3 other programs.
  • Heads up: One of them, Apple Health Expansion, was closed to new people as of December 2025.
  • This site: It never asks about your immigration status.

Who the change affects

From Oct. 1, 2026, the rules for federally funded Medicaid are narrower for refugees, asylees, and other noncitizen adults.

HCA estimated that more than 30,000 people enrolled in Washington could be affected.

Other programs HCA names

HCA names these programs for people who lose federally funded coverage:

  • Apple Health Expansion
  • Emergency Medical
  • Pregnancy or After-Pregnancy Coverage

Apple Health Expansion was closed to new people

Apple Health Expansion is paid for by the state. As of December 2025, it was closed to new people. Its enrollment limit had been met.

Check HCA's page for the latest: Apple Health Expansion.
