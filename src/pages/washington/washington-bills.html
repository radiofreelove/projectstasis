Medical bills from before you apply for Apple Health

Apple Health may pay some bills from before you apply. On Jan. 1, 2027, that time gets shorter.

The short version

  • Before Jan. 1, 2027: Apple Health may pay bills from up to 3 months before the month you apply.
  • From Jan. 1, 2027: 1 month for Apple Health for Adults. 2 months for all other Apple Health programs.
  • What changes: Coverage for past bills gets shorter on Jan. 1, 2027.

What this means for you
