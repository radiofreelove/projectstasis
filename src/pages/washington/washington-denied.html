Turned down for Apple Health? You have options.

This page explains what to do if the state says no or your coverage ends.

The short version

  • Ask for a hearing: If your application is denied, you have 90 days to ask.
  • Keep coverage: To keep coverage during an appeal, ask fast. The deadline is short.
  • Missed a renewal: You have 90 days to send it and get coverage back.
  • Open all year: Apple Health enrollment is open all year.

Check your own letter

Your letter lists your deadlines. If your letter and this page differ, follow your letter.

The deadlines on this page come from state rules. A person on this project has not yet read those rules from start to finish.

Ask for a hearing

  • Who can ask: A person who got a decision letter and disagrees with it.
  • Who to ask: The Office of Administrative Hearings. Ask online, or call 1-800-583-8271.
  • Deadline: If your application is denied, you have 90 days to ask for a hearing.

The Health Care Authority (HCA) explains the steps on its page: File an appeal: Apple Health (Medicaid).

Keep coverage while you appeal

To keep coverage while an appeal runs, your hearing request must arrive by one of these dates:

  • The 10th day after the notice was sent
  • The last day of the month before the change takes effect

If a health plan made the decision

  1. Go through the health plan's own grievance and appeal process first.
  2. The plan sends a notice of resolution. Your hearing request must arrive within 120 calendar days of that notice.

If coverage ended at a renewal

Did coverage end because a renewal was not finished? You have 90 days from the day coverage ended to send the completed renewal.

Until Jan. 1, 2027, the state rule says coverage comes back with no gap, if you are still eligible. A new version of the rule starts Jan. 1, 2027. It keeps the 90 days. It says you do not need a new application. It no longer says "with no gap."

Applying

Apple Health enrollment is open all year. How to apply

If you meet the rules now, or a reason fits you now, you may qualify. Take the 1-minute check.
