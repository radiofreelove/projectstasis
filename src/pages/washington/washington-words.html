Words on your Apple Health letters

Plain meanings for words Washington uses. Listed from A to Z.

Apple Health
The name Washington uses for Medicaid.
Apple Health Classic
Apple Health for people 65 or older, people who are blind or have a disability, and people with Medicare. You apply through DSHS.
Apple Health for Adults
The coverage group the work rules apply to. A state rule, WAC 182-505-0250, sets out its tests. Who the rules are for
Automatic renewal
The state renews your coverage from data it already has. It does not ask you for proof. Federal papers call this an ex parte renewal. The Health Care Authority (HCA) says its system still tries this first.
Community engagement
The federal name for the work or activity rules. HCA calls them work requirements.
Exemption
HCA's word for a reason you do not have to meet the work rules. See every reason
Medically frail
People who are medically frail do not have to meet the work rules. More about health conditions
MEDS (Medical Eligibility Determination Services)
The HCA office behind the 1-800-562-3022 phone line. It is for MAGI Apple Health cases.
Post Eligibility Review (PER)
A check after you apply or renew. It may ask for proof of income, citizenship, or lawful presence.
Washington Healthplanfinder
The website where adults under 65 without Medicare apply or renew. Its address is wahealthplanfinder.org.
Work requirements
HCA's name for the new federal work rules. They start Jan. 1, 2027. Read them in plain words
