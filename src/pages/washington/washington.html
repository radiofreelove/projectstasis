Applying for Apple Health? New rules start Jan. 1, 2027.

Why this guide exists: The facts about the new Apple Health rules are spread across many official pages and papers. This guide puts what applicants need in one place, in plain words. Each page lists its official sources.

Have medical bills? Read this before Jan. 1, 2027.

Before Jan. 1, 2027, Apple Health may pay bills from up to 3 months before the month you apply.

On Jan. 1, 2027, that time gets shorter. For Apple Health for Adults, it is 1 month.

More about past medical bills

The short version

  • The name: Apple Health is Washington's name for Medicaid.
  • What's new: New federal work rules for Apple Health for Adults start Jan. 1, 2027.
  • Right now: The Health Care Authority (HCA) says there is nothing you need to do right now.
  • Who: Only people in Apple Health for Adults. That means ages 19 to 64, no Medicare, living in Washington, with low income.
  • Records first: Washington checks its own data first. HCA estimated that it could check about 64 percent of people in Apple Health for Adults this way, or find they are exempt.
  • Shorter coverage: Coverage periods go from 12 months to 6 months. That starts at a person's first renewal in 2027.
  • Notices: HCA sends notices by mail or email. Keep your contact information up to date.

Meet the rules any one way

  • Income: Your household has at least $580 in income a month.
  • School: You are in an educational program at least half-time.
  • 80 hours: A work program, community service, or a mix of activities. The total is at least 80 hours a month.

The rules, in plain words

You may not have to if you

  • Are pregnant, or in the 12 months after a pregnancy
  • Are a parent, caretaker, or guardian of a child under 14
  • Are medically frail
  • Are American Indian or Alaska Native
  • Are a foster youth, or a former foster youth under 26

Many people do not have to meet the rule at all. HCA's fact sheet calls these reasons exemptions. See every reason

What to do

  1. Take the 1-minute check. Your answers stay on your device.
  2. Apply online, by phone, on paper, or in person. Apple Health enrollment is open all year.
  3. You may tell the state your own income, work, or exemption status. The state accepts what you tell it.

Bottom line

This guide says "you may." Only Washington decides who meets the rules or is exempt.
