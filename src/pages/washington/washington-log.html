Hours log you can print

Write down your hours. Keep the page in case the state asks.

Please read

  • This is not an Apple Health form. It is for your own records.
  • This log runs by month. Washington tests the month before a renewal date.
  • When you apply, the rule looks at the month before you apply.
  • The goal is at least 80 hours in a month.
  • Keep pay stubs, school schedules, and community service records with this log. The Health Care Authority (HCA) may ask for proof after a renewal.
