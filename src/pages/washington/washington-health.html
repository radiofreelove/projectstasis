Have a health condition?

A disability or a health condition may mean you do not have to meet the new Apple Health rules.

The short version

  • Medically frail: People who are medically frail do not have to meet the work rules.
  • Treatment counts: People in treatment for alcohol or substance use disorders do not have to meet the work rules.
  • A court order helps: A federal court struck down an extra federal test for the states that sued. Washington is one of them.

What federal law counts as medically frail

  • Being blind or disabled
  • Having a substance use disorder
  • Having a disabling mental disorder
  • Having a physical, intellectual, or developmental disability that makes at least one daily living activity much harder
  • Having a serious or complex medical condition

In the news

The federal rule had added one more test. It asked if the condition makes it much harder to comply with the work rules. A federal court struck that test down for the states that sued. Washington is one of those states.
