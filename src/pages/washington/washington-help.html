Free help with Apple Health, and other languages

People who can help you apply, for free, and in your language.

The short version

  • Call the state: The Health Care Authority (HCA) answers questions about your case at 1-800-562-3022.
  • Free helpers: Navigators and other local helpers can help you. Their help is free.
  • Your language: Language help is free.
  • Booklets: Apple Health client booklets come in English and 14 other languages.

Phone numbers

HCA, for questions about your Apple Health case
Medical Eligibility Determination Services (MEDS): 1-800-562-3022. Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. Pacific time. This line is for MAGI Apple Health cases.
Washington Healthplanfinder, to apply and for language help
Customer Support Center: 1-855-923-4633. TTY: 1-855-627-9604.
DSHS, for Apple Health Classic
1-877-501-2233. Apple Health Classic is for people 65 or older, people who are blind or have a disability, and people with Medicare.
Hearings
Office of Administrative Hearings: 1-800-583-8271

HCA lists its phone lines on its page: Contact Apple Health (Medicaid).

Free local help

  • Navigators
  • Community-based enrollment specialists
  • Apple Health Community Connectors

A navigator can help you in person. The help is free. HCA's page has more: Apply for or renew coverage.

Help in your language

  • Language help is free. It includes staff who speak your language, interpreters, and translated printed papers.
  • Washington Healthplanfinder gives language help at 1-855-923-4633.
  • HCA says each of its fact sheets about the new federal law comes in 15 languages. We have not checked which languages.

Apple Health booklets

HCA's Apple Health client booklets come in English and 14 other languages: Amharic, Arabic, Burmese, Cambodian (Khmer), Chinese, Farsi (Persian), Korean, Laotian, Punjabi, Russian, Somali, Spanish, Tigrigna, Ukrainian, and Vietnamese.
