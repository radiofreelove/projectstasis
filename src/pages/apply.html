How to apply for OHP

There are 4 ways to apply. Pick the one that is easiest for you.

The short version

  • Online: benefits.oregon.gov
  • By phone: 800-699-9075 (TTY 711), Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • In person: at an ODHS office, or with a free community partner.
  • On paper: the OHP application, form 7210, in 17 languages.
  • Help is free: Community partners do not charge.

Apply online

Go to benefits.oregon.gov. Make a ONE account. Then apply.

Some state pages say ONE.Oregon.gov instead. That is the same ONE system.

Trouble with the website? Call ONE technical support at 833-978-1073.

Apply by phone

Call ONE Customer Service at 800-699-9075. TTY users can dial 711. Lines are open Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific time.

In Spanish, call 800-743-9182. In Russian, call 833-687-1508. See all language lines PDF

Apply in person

  • Find an ODHS office near you
  • Find a free community partner near you

Apply on paper

Download the OHP application (form 7210) PDF. It comes in 17 languages. Get it in another language.

Heads up about the paper form

The paper form is from May 2020. It does not ask about the new rules. OHP may send you a letter asking about them.

Questions about where to send it? Call 800-699-9075.

What to have ready

  • Facts about each person in your household.
  • Income for each person: jobs, self-employment, and other money coming in.
  • Any other health insurance you have.
  • For the new rules: your work, school, or volunteer hours for the month before you apply, or the reason you don't have to meet the rules.

What happens next

  1. OHP checks its own records first, like payroll and school records.
  2. If OHP can confirm everything, you get a letter with its decision. It is called a Notice of Eligibility.
  3. If OHP needs more, you get a letter called a Request for Information. You have 35 days to reply. How to answer a letter

Over the income limit?

  • Check the OHP income limits chart PDF.
  • OHP Bridge covers adults with income just above the OHP limit. Members pay nothing.
  • You can also look at private plans at OregonHealthCare.gov or call 855-268-3767.
