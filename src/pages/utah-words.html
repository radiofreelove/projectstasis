Words on your Utah Medicaid letters

Plain meanings for words Utah Medicaid uses. Listed from A to Z.

Adult Expansion Medicaid
Utah's Medicaid program for adults ages 19 to 64 with low income. It started in 2020.
Community engagement requirement
Utah's name for the work or activity rules.
DHHS (Department of Health and Human Services)
The state agency that runs Utah Medicaid.
DWS (Department of Workforce Services)
The state agency that decides who qualifies for Utah Medicaid.
Employment center
A DWS office. You can apply there.
Exclusion and exception
Utah's words for reasons you do not have to meet the work or activity rules. See every reason.
Fair hearing
A way to challenge a decision. A judge who was not part of the decision hears your side.
Half-time
A school schedule your school calls half-time or more.
Lookback period
The time the state checks. When you apply, it is the month before you applied.
Medically frail
Utah's words for people with a disability or a health condition that makes daily tasks hard. More about health conditions.
myCase
Your online account with DWS. You can apply, check your renewal date, and upload papers there.
Renewal, or review
When the state checks again whether you still qualify. Starting Jan. 1, 2027, adults in Adult Expansion Medicaid and TAM get one every 6 months.
Retroactive coverage
Coverage for care you got before you applied.
TAM (Targeted Adult Medicaid)
A Utah program for some adults with very low income, such as people who have been homeless or who need mental health or substance use treatment. Utah's glossary says TAM ends June 30, 2027, and its members move to Adult Expansion Medicaid.
Work or activity rules
The new rules that start Jan. 1, 2027. Read them in plain words.
