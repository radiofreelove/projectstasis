Accessibility

This site is built to be easy to use for as many people as possible.

The short version

  • Standard: Built to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Colors meet the stricter AAA level.
  • Easy to think through: Built with W3C advice for people with cognitive and learning disabilities.
  • Make it yours: Change text size, spacing, font, colors, and how much shows. Look for "Display settings" at the top.
  • Tested so far: Automated checks only. Testing with people who use assistive technology has not happened yet.

What we did

  • One main idea per page. The page's purpose is in the first lines.
  • Short sentences and plain words. We aim for a 6th grade reading level and check it with a tool.
  • The same layout on every page. Nothing moves, blinks, or plays on its own.
  • No time limits. The check lets you go back and change answers.
  • Big buttons and links that are easy to tap.
  • A thick yellow and black outline shows where you are when you use a keyboard.
  • Works at 400% zoom and on small phones.
  • "Just the key points" mode hides extra details.
  • Colors come from the Oregon Department of Human Services' published palette. Every text color pair is checked for contrast.
  • Pages print cleanly, with web addresses shown.

What we cannot control

  • State forms and PDFs we link to are made by the state. Some may be hard to use with a screen reader. You can ask OHP for another format for free at 800-699-9075.
  • We have not translated this site. We link to the state's own translated forms and language lines instead.

Tell us what is not working

Please tell us on GitHub. Reports are public, so do not share personal details.
