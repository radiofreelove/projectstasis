Turned down for Utah Medicaid? You have options.

This page explains what to do if the state says no or your coverage ends.

The short version

  • Apply again: You can apply again.
  • Ask for a hearing: You have 90 days from the date on the notice.
  • Keep benefits: To keep medical benefits while you wait, ask within 15 days of the date on the notice. If you lose, you have to pay them back.
  • Free legal help: Call Utah Legal Services.

Apply again

You can apply again. Utah says there is no waiting period after something changes, like your income or who lives with you.

For the work or activity rules, the state looks at the month before your new application.

How to apply

Ask for a hearing

A fair hearing is a way to challenge a decision. A judge who was not part of the decision hears your side.

  • Who to ask: Hearings about who qualifies go to the Department of Workforce Services (DWS).
  • Deadline: 90 days from the date on the notice you disagree with.
  • Keep benefits: Ask within 15 days of the date on the notice to keep medical benefits during the hearing. If the decision goes against you, you have to pay that help back.
  • How it works: Hearings are held by phone. A free interpreter can be arranged.
  • Bring someone: You can bring a lawyer or another person to speak for you.

How to ask

  • Form: Hearing request, form 490 PDF, or in Spanish PDF
  • Mail: Department of Workforce Services, Fair Hearings, PO Box 143245, Salt Lake City, UT 84114-3245
  • Fax: 877-824-6534
  • Phone: 877-837-3247
  • Email: fairhearings@utah.gov
  • In person: any DWS employment center

The form says not to send a hearing request through myCase.

Free legal help

Utah Legal Services

The state's hearing form lists Utah Legal Services for free legal help.

Call 801-328-8891 or 800-662-4245.

Utah Legal Services website

Need help reading a notice? Take Care Utah can explain it and help you answer. Its help is free. Call 801-433-2299.

Other coverage

You can look at private health plans at HealthCare.gov or call 1-800-318-2596.

Care while you sort it out

  • Utah lists free and low-cost clinics, dental care, and help with medicine costs. See Utah's list.
  • 211 Utah lists help by county.
