Hours log you can print

Write down your hours. Keep the page in case the state asks.

Please read

  • This is not a Utah Medicaid form. It is for your own records.
  • The state decides what proof it accepts.
  • Utah has its own form for reporting, form 742. Ask the state for it.
  • When you apply, the state looks at the month before you apply.
  • The goal is 80 hours in a month. Work, job training, volunteering, and school all add up.
