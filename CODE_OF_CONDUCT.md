# Code of Conduct

The Stasis Project exists for people applying for the Oregon Health Plan (OHP) and the people who help them. Many of them are dealing with illness, disability, money worries, or immigration questions. Every space connected to this project (issues, pull requests, comments, and any chat or meeting) must be safe for them.

## Expected

- Treat people with respect and assume good faith.
- Use the names and pronouns people give.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Protect privacy: never share anyone's personal or case details, such as a name, case number, health, or immigration status.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, threats, or insults of any kind.
- Shaming anyone for needing help, for their health, income, or work, or for how they ask a question.
- Asking anyone about their immigration status, or sharing private information without consent.
- Content that demeans people for disability, health, income, race, ethnicity, national origin, immigration status, religion, age, body, gender identity, or sexual orientation.
- Spam or bad-faith "corrections" meant to mislead readers.

## Reporting

Open an issue that says only: "I need to report a conduct problem." Do not put details or a way to reach you in it. Issues are public, and so is your GitHub account name. A maintainer will reply in that issue with a private way to talk, and reports are handled privately from there. You do not need to share personal details to report.

You can also report a comment straight to GitHub: open the comment's menu (the three dots) and choose **Report content**.

## What happens next

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn, or block anyone who breaks this code, at their discretion and in proportion to the harm. Blocking can be immediate for threats or for sharing someone's private information.

This code is adapted from The Strand Project's ([CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](https://github.com/radiofreelove/projectstrand/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)), so the two projects ask the same of everyone. See [docs/decisions/0012-shared-with-strand.md](docs/decisions/0012-shared-with-strand.md).
